About Veeto LTD

From a passion for games to mission-critical cloud.

Headquartered in Cardiff and founded by Chartered IT Professional (CITP) Vito Caresimo, Veeto LTD brings over 30 years of hands-on enterprise engineering across UK and European public and private sectors. Born in 1970 with proud Welsh-Italian roots (and fluent in both English and Italian), Vito turned an early passion for video games and simulation hardware into a multifaceted career spanning mission-critical IT infrastructure, hybrid cloud architecture, and game development.

Why “Veeto”?

The name comes from the Italian name Vito. In Italian the “i” is pronounced “ee” — Vee-to — so the company was spelt Veeto to say it exactly the way it sounds.

On the enterprise side, Veeto LTD specialises in bridging the gap between foundational on-premises estates (Windows Server, Active Directory, VMware vSphere, Hyper-V, Citrix) and modern Microsoft Azure cloud platforms using Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, Bicep, ARM) and Azure DevOps CI/CD. Whether leading a 321-server migration programme across 23 business services or securing regulated defence and financial workloads, Veeto LTD delivers resilient, repeatable, and audit-ready solutions.
